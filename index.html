Hurricane Season bot

A trading bot that prices storm damage live and trades while the market is slow to catch up.

1,800storms studied
+$5–9Kper season, holdout replay
7thplace, Systematic Equities
Overview

Our team’s bot for the Systematic Equities case at the 2026 Cornell Trading Competition. Hurricanes hit a set of companies, and the exchange reprices them slowly while a storm is happening. The bot works out each company’s fair value from the live storm feed, trades toward it while the market lags, and gets out once the market catches up.

What we built
  • Research on 600 simulated seasons showing the settlement prices are exact formulas of storm damage, and that the market only lags mid-storm
  • A live “nowcast” model of damage and outages from the storm feed, combining news sources that are each only 40 to 70% reliable
  • A holdout-only backtester with simulated spreads, latency and order-rate limits
  • ETF-vs-basket arbitrage, risk limits, kill switches, and a live dashboard for the team
Stack
Python · WebSockets · scikit-learn · pytest

Grandmaster Council

An AI chess coach that finds the moves that actually cost you the game.

Demo: pick a grandmaster voice, play, and get coached on the moves that actually mattered.

4thof 70 teams
1,000+applicants
14KGM games
Overview

Built with a team of five at Point72’s NYC office. Most chess engines tell you a move was bad. Grandmaster Council tells you why, by asking what would have happened if you’d played something else. It flags only the mistakes that do real damage later in the game (50+ centipawns downstream), and explains them through four grandmaster “coaches” built from 14K of their real games.

The four GM engines’ style fingerprints: how much each one weights each part of the evaluation, learned from their games.

What I built
  • Counterfactual coaching engine based on Pearl’s three levels of causal reasoning
  • Flask and Next.js app, with Stockfish as the referee for grading moves
  • Four GM personalities learned from PGN data
  • An adversarial AI workflow where separate Builder, Critic, Analyst and Judge sessions checked each other’s work
Stack
Python · Flask · Next.js · Stockfish · Minimax · Claude API

Overview

A live trading bot for Kalshi, the prediction market exchange. It quotes both sides of a contract and earns the spread, with strict position limits so a bad day stays small.

What I built

Cornell Data Science · Jan to May 2026

WorldWideWeb

A world model of the web, so AI agents can practice offline.

8×cheaper training
69Ktraining triples
28→8 GBmodel memory
Overview

Training web agents on real websites is slow and expensive. We built a model that predicts how a page changes after a click or keystroke, so agents can train against the model instead.

What I built
  • PyTorch world model that predicts DOM changes
  • Synthetic data engine: filtered 91K triples down to 69K, with Llama 3 8B labeling the trajectories
  • GSPO policy training with 4-bit QLoRA, taking a 7B model from 28GB to 8GB
Stack
Python · PyTorch · model-based RL · Docker

Beloved Dunks

A pair of Dunks that retells Toni Morrison’s Beloved in symbols.

The project

Made for AP English Language and Composition. Instead of an essay on Beloved, I painted it onto a pair of Dunks. Each detail stands for something in the book: the winged skull from the gravestone, gray for Sethe’s sacrifice, red for Beloved’s blood, the chokecherry tree for the scars Sethe carries, and cowprint and milk for the dehumanization of slavery.
